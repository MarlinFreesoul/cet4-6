# Section A intervention and transfer design

## Write operational interventions

Use:

`trigger → action → visible trace → target mechanism`

Examples:

- `When two words seem semantically possible → write the quick slot signature → cross out form/frame failures → reduce SLOT and VAL errors.`
- `When the passage model changes in a later paragraph → mark “rescan” and revisit earlier provisional blanks → reduce MODEL-driven errors.`
- `Before final submission → reread every restored sentence and mark checked blanks → reduce CHECK and BANK cascades.`

Reject “improve vocabulary,” “read more carefully,” or “learn grammar” as interventions.

## Rank candidates

Score 0–2 on:

- recurrence;
- explanatory reach;
- observability;
- transfer value;
- cognitive/time cost, reverse scored.

Choose no more than two. A high-value intervention must target the observed topology, not merely sound useful.

## Separate execution and efficacy

| Execution | Target outcome | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| low | unchanged | Efficacy unknown; simplify or cue the action |
| high | improved | Supports the mechanism and intervention |
| high | unchanged | Diagnosis or action may be insufficient |
| high | worse | Inspect cognitive load, misapplication, and material mismatch |

## Experiment types

### Delayed retrieval

Reuse the reviewed passage after a delay. Measure recovery of words, chunks, sentence structure, and reasoning. Do not claim transfer.

### Near transfer

Use an unseen CET4 Reading Section A passage matching the official fingerprint. Keep scope mode and source quality comparable.

### Format transfer

Do not jump automatically to CET6, careful reading, IELTS, or a different cloze format. Create or invoke another adapter after near-transfer evidence exists.

## Default Section A metrics

Select only metrics tied to the hypothesis:

- raw accuracy / 10;
- candidate-set overlap / 10;
- misplaced correct candidates;
- selected distractors and missed correct candidates;
- form/frame-invalid placements;
- swaps and cascade length;
- first-pass model accuracy and model-update point;
- guesses and confidence calibration;
- active time and scope mode;
- intervention opportunities and executions;
- target-code recurrence;
- stable-correct proportion.

## Next-experiment contract

Record:

- hypothesis;
- unseen paper identity and opening words;
- official-format check;
- invariant conditions;
- one or two changed actions;
- execution traces;
- process metrics;
- outcome metrics;
- support criterion;
- falsification/revision criterion;
- contamination handling.

Prefer two unseen comparable passages before promoting a learner hypothesis into a stable rule.

## Project-based rhythm

Use one complete cycle as the unit:

`unseen Section A → freeze evidence → topology and causal diagnosis → one or two interventions → delayed retrieval → unseen near transfer → learner-model update`

Do not replace this with fixed daily word quotas. Use spaced retrieval for the exact contextual senses, chunks, frames, and reading actions exposed by the experiment.
