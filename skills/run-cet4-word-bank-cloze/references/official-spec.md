## Official question-type contract

Under the current published specification, CET4 reading contains:

- vocabulary comprehension: word-bank cloze, 10 items, 5% of the whole test;
- long reading: matching, 10 items, 10%;
- careful reading: multiple choice, 10 items, 20%;
- 40 minutes shared by the entire reading component.

For vocabulary comprehension specifically:

- one passage;
- 200–250 words;
- 10 deleted words;
- 15 candidate words supplied after the passage;
- select the correct candidates based on passage comprehension;
- restore the passage;
- stated construct: understanding and using vocabulary in passage context.

The recurring paper directions also state that no candidate may be used more than once.

## Broader official reading abilities

The syllabus describes the reading component as assessing acquisition of written information, including:

- main ideas, facts, and details;
- explicit and implicit information;
- author viewpoint and attitude;
- contextual inference of word and phrase meaning;
- sentence relations such as cause, comparison, and condition;
- lexical and grammatical cohesion across a passage;
- suitable reading strategies.

Do not claim that every broad reading ability is weighted equally in Section A. The type-specific primary construct remains contextual vocabulary understanding and use; the broader abilities are supporting diagnostic dimensions when the blank depends on them.

## Timing and score boundaries

- The official 40-minute limit covers all three reading task types together.
- There is no official standalone Section A time limit in the cited specification.
- CET reported scores are norm-referenced.
- Reading is reported as one section, not as a separate official Section A score.
- Record `x/10` and the official 5% weight; do not claim a fixed 710-scale value per blank.

## Repository compatibility audit

The project corpus was checked across 12 third-party compiled papers from 2024-12, 2025-06, 2025-12, and 2026-06, three sets per administration. Every reading Section A used the same operational fingerprint:

- ten blanks;
- fifteen A–O candidates;
- each candidate used at most once;
- items 26–35;
- complete-passage restoration.

This corpus supports reuse across those archived papers, but it is not an official guarantee about future formats. Treat the source as third-party material and recheck official specifications when format drift appears.

## Format-drift rule

Mark `FORMAT_DRIFT` and pause the standard protocol if any of these change:

- task is not CET4 reading vocabulary comprehension;
- blank count is not 10;
- candidate count is not 15;
- candidates can be reused;
- task no longer restores one passage;
- official site publishes a superseding specification.

Continue only after recording the changed contract and adapting the Skill.
